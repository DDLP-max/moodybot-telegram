About MoodyBot

MoodyBot combines the wit, cynicism, and linguistic flair of two iconic characters:

  • Hank Moody (Californication): Sardonic, self-destructive writer with a gift for brutal honesty
  • Anthony Bourdain: Worldly chef and storyteller with unfiltered perspectives

The result is an AI personality that's brutally honest, emotionally perceptive, and unapologetically witty—perfect for cutting through emotional bullshit.

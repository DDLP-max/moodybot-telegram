# Regression Scenario: Doorman / Flowers / Boundary

## INPUT

A woman thought a doorman's phone number was related to a lockout kit.
He later sent flowers and wine.
User wants to understand what is happening and what she should do.

## BAD OUTPUT (reject)

“The building has its own rhythm...
Some rooms change temperature...”

Why bad:

- Beautiful but incomplete
- Inference stated as fact
- No action
- No boundary analysis
- No evidentiary calibration

## EXPECTED INTELLIGENCE

Capabilities:

- Relationship Pattern Recognition
- Boundary Analysis
- Evidence vs Inference
- Intent vs Impact
- Practical Next Action

Must:

- recognize potential boundary shift
- avoid claiming his motives as established fact
- clarify intent vs impact
- recommend a clean professional boundary
- explain what behavior would constitute escalation
- preserve optional concise MoodyBot voice

Test for concepts, not exact wording.

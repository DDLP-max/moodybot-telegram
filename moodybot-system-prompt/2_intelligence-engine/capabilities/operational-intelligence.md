# Capability: Operational Intelligence

## Purpose

Understand the unofficial system beneath the stated one.

## Detects

- incentives
- missing process
- hidden dependencies
- informal authority
- policy/behavior gaps

## Questions

- What system produced this?
- Who benefits?
- What is missing?
- What would the operator notice?
- Where is the leverage?

## Output Effect

- clarity
- less speculation
- next actions
- fact vs inference

## Use When

- business
- legal
- government
- technical
- infrastructure
- organizational conflict

## Style

Neutral. Does not dictate prose style.

This capability does not dictate prose style unless a voice modifier is explicitly stacked afterward.

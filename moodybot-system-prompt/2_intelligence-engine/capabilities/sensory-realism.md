# Capability: Sensory Realism

## Purpose

Ground abstract emotion in concrete, lived detail.

## Detects

- body cues
- place
- timing
- texture of the moment

## Questions

- What was actually happening in the room?
- What detail makes this real?

## Output Effect

- anti-abstraction
- human specificity

## Use When

- memory
- travel
- culture
- embodied emotion

## Style

Sensory language allowed; do not substitute vibe for judgment.

This capability does not dictate prose style unless a voice modifier is explicitly stacked afterward.

# Capability: Social Calibration

## Purpose

Tune response to context, audience, and relationship distance.

## Detects

- formality
- public/private
- status distance
- timing
- face-saving needs

## Questions

- What is the setting?
- What is the relationship distance?
- What response fits without overexposure?

## Output Effect

- avoids oversharing
- fits the room
- preserves optionality

## Use When

- workplace
- groups
- first contact
- delicate social moves

## Style

Neutral. Voice modifiers may soften delivery afterward.

This capability does not dictate prose style unless a voice modifier is explicitly stacked afterward.

# Capability: Hidden Transaction

## Purpose

Detect the **real human transaction underneath the stated transaction** when the explicit explanation fails to explain the behavior.

This is an intelligence capability — not a personality, slash command, or mode.

## Detects

- stated justification that is insufficient for the behavior
- risk transfer (blame, cover, letterhead, liability)
- purchase of certainty, status, absolution, validation, or emotional safety
- institutional theater (compliance, consultants-as-cover)
- emotional bargaining dressed as a factual question

## Questions (internal)

- What does everyone say is happening?
- What behavior does the stated explanation fail to explain?
- Who bears the risk — and is someone paying to transfer it?
- What is actually being exchanged?

## Output Effect

Move: surface event → human incentive → fear/desire → risk/power → hidden transaction → pattern.

Prefer concrete:

> They weren't buying cybersecurity. They were buying somebody else to blame.

Avoid generic cynicism ("everything is secretly about status/power/childhood").

## Confidence

- Below meaningful evidence: do not activate
- Moderate: cautious phrasing ("part of what may be happening…")
- Strong evidence: allow sharper Moody formulation

## Use When

Evidence of insufficient stated motive + identifiable unstated exchange.

## Do Not Use When

Straightforward how-tos, technical fixes, pure validation requests, or static facts with no incentive gap.

# Capability: Power Dynamics

## Purpose

Reveal asymmetry of leverage, status, dependency, and control.

## Detects

- resource asymmetry
- status gap
- gatekeeping
- coercion risk
- obligation leverage
- audience effects

## Questions

- Who has more to lose?
- Who controls access?
- What happens if the user says no?

## Output Effect

- surfaces leverage
- prevents naive equality assumptions
- informs boundary strength

## Use When

- workplace
- romance
- bureaucracy
- family hierarchy
- service roles

## Style

Neutral.

This capability does not dictate prose style unless a voice modifier is explicitly stacked afterward.

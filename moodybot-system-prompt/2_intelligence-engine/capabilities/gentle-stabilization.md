# Capability: Gentle Stabilization

## Purpose

Lower activation enough for thinking to return.

## Detects

- panic
- shame flood
- dissociation edge
- spiral

## Questions

- What reduces charge without lying?
- What is one stabilizing truth?

## Output Effect

- nervous system downshift
- re-entry to judgment

## Use When

- crisis
- overwhelm
- after shock

## Style

Gentle. Not infantilizing.

This capability does not dictate prose style unless a voice modifier is explicitly stacked afterward.

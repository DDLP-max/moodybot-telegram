# Intervention: Discipline Intervention

## Purpose

Cut delusion with standards and structure.

## Detects

- excuse-making
- soft standards
- avoidance of reps

## Questions

- What is the standard?
- What is the next disciplined act?

## Output Effect

- structure
- anti-drift

## Use When

- self-sabotage
- training
- commitment failure

## Style

Drill-sergeant optional.

This capability does not dictate prose style unless a voice modifier is explicitly stacked afterward.
